# Launch kit: the story, the strategy templates, and what a front end wires up

For whoever writes the site, the announcement and the launch form. Three parts: what we say (and what we must not),
the templates a creator picks from, and the contract surface behind each screen. Numbers come from
[`rule-backtest/`](./rule-backtest/README.md); positioning and the v1 / v2 split from [`ROADMAP.md`](./ROADMAP.md).

## 1. The story

**One line.** A meme coin with a working stock treasury inside. / 每个币里都有一个替持币人干活的股票金库。

**Thirty seconds.** Anyone can launch a coin against a tokenised stock on Robinhood Chain in one transaction, with no
capital. Every buy burns some of the coin. Every sell pays a tax in the stock itself -- real GME, real NVDA -- and most
of it goes into a treasury that nobody can withdraw from: not the creator, not us. The treasury trades that stock by a
rule the creator published at launch and nobody can change, and spends what it earns buying the coin back and burning it.
The pool's liquidity is locked for good. Supply only falls; the treasury is always on chain for anyone to read.

**Against what people already know on this chain**

| | pons (read from its source) | Artificial Inu, $AI (its site) | this launchpad |
|---|---|---|---|
| what it is | a launchpad | one coin | a launchpad |
| quoted in a stock | optional | NVDA | always, the listed stock |
| where the fee goes | 30% protocol / 35% creator / 35% "buy-back" that is locked 5 years, not burned | buys: 80% to a vault, 20% to a receiver. Sells: half burned, half locked | buys: burned. Sells, in the stock: ~70% treasury / 20% protocol / 10% creator |
| what the vault does | -- | "only grows" | holds or trades by a published, immutable rule; profit buys back and burns |
| who controls the vault | -- | not disclosed | nobody can move it. The protocol Safe may only point its votes (reserved; see below) |

**Three lines for three audiences**

- Meme traders: *pons, but the fee works for holders instead of walking out of the door.*
- The GME / CRCL crowd: *diamond hands, as a contract -- a treasury that is forbidden to sell below cost.*
- Stock people: *a permissionless MicroStrategy for any listed stock, funded by trading tax instead of debt.*

**What every page must be able to show** (all already on chain): shares the treasury holds (raw amount x
`uiMultiplier` / 1e18) and their USDG value; share of supply burned; stock spent on buy-backs; sell tax over the last
24h; the operations feed (`LotBooked`, `ProfitTaken`, `Stopped`, `Buyback`, `Swept`). And, platform-wide, **"all
treasuries together hold X GME / Y NVDA"**.

**What we never say**

| never | because |
|---|---|
| "the strategy beats holding the stock" | across 192 stocks the median rule is at or below holding; see the templates |
| NAV, price per token, discount / premium | nothing is redeemable; a holder has no claim on the treasury |
| ETF, fund, yield, dividend to holders | it is none of those. Dividends reach the STOCK token through its multiplier; holders of the coin get buy-backs |
| "no owner" (bare) | true of the token and the treasury's assets. The hook's two payout addresses can move (a creator's only after a public, vetoable 14 days); the Safe can halt the rule through the calendar; and it holds the vote-delegation hatch |
| "your shares vote" | tokenised stock carries no vote on this chain today. Say: *the right to direct the treasuries' votes is reserved to the protocol, in the contract, for the day the issuer opens one* |

One sentence always goes with the pitch: **no volume, no tax, nothing turns.** The coin's price is the market's
business; what the contracts guarantee is where the fee goes.

## 2. Strategy templates

A rule is five numbers -- first and second take-profit, the dip that re-enters, an optional stop, the share of the
reserve a dip spends -- frozen at launch. The form should offer these and let an expert edit them, inside the floors the
contracts enforce (take-profit and dip at least twice `maxSlippage + pool fee`: 2.1% on a 0.05% pool, 2.6% at 0.30%).

Each was replayed on 192 stocks, hourly, two to three years, as it happened AND backwards (same volatility, opposite
drift). The figure is stock gained or given away against simply sitting on the tax; the worse direction is shown,
because nobody knows which one comes next.

| template | tp1 / tp2 | dip | stop | lot | range-bound stocks | mild trend | strong trend | tax turned into buy-backs | for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Diamond hands** -- sells only above +655% | 65,535 bps, the most a `uint16` holds; `tp2 = 0` | -- | none | -- | 0% | 0% | 0% | 0% | the pure "vault only grows" coin. No buy-back, ever: the burn comes from the buy tax alone |
| **Trend holder** | 30% / 60% | 3% | none | 50% | -0.3% | -1.0% | -2.9% | 42% | large caps that run: AMZN, META, NVDA. The default |
| **Range grid** | 20% / 40% | 15% | none | 20% | **+7.3%** | -0.8% | -25% | 28% | violent and going nowhere: CRCL, USAR, GME |
| **Scalper** | 5% / 10% | 5% | none | 20% | +1.1% | -7.8% | -27% | 30% | only a stock the creator is sure is flat. Busy feed, worst in a trend |
| **Take-profit + stop** | 15% / 30% | 10% | 12% | 50% | -6.0% | -15% | -33% | 27% | a stock that has round-tripped a big move (RBLX: a 10% stop took it from -10% to +10%). Costs everywhere else |
| **Cash-out** -- sells, never rebuys | 20% / 40% | unreachable | none | -- | +5.8% | -5.4% | -34% | 21% | NOT offered in v1: 43% of everything received ends as USDG the rule can never spend. It waits for the v2 payout ratio |

Read it as the form should say it: **the template matters less than the stock.** Range-bound and volatile is where a
rule earns; on a stock that trends, the honest choice is the one that trades least. "Trend holder" is the default because
even on strongly trending stocks its median is about -3%, while it still turns 42% of the tax into buy-backs; "Diamond hands" is the only rule that (almost -- see below) cannot
lose, and the only one that never buys back.


**"Never sells" is not quite never.** `tp1Bps` is a `uint16`, so the highest take-profit a creator can set is 65,535 bps:
a lot sells when the stock reaches 7.55x its cost. That is out of reach for most stocks over most horizons and the
template's numbers above assume it never fires -- but SNDK went 37x in nineteen months, so say "sells only above +655%",
not "never sells". (Until the `HedgeFunMath` refactor this was different and worse: the threshold was computed as
`1e4 + tp1Bps` IN `uint16`, which overflows above 55,535 bps, so a rule set that high could not sell at ANY price -- every
`takeProfit` panicked. Nothing was ever deployed with it.)

Not in v1 (see ROADMAP): martingale and ladders (blocks), a launch-price table, a payout ratio, dividend buy-back. A
martingale lifted the median on range-bound names (USAR +12% -> +18..22%) and deepened the worst window on one with a
squeeze in its past (GME -19% -> -33%): an option, never a default.

**Closure trading is one more frozen choice on the form**: `bandBpsPerHour`, at most the stock's `bandCeiling`. Offer the
measured value, not a slider to the ceiling -- 10 for CRCL, 5 for GME / AMZN / META, 0 or 5 for USAR (ROADMAP and
DEPLOYMENT have the numbers). Say what it buys: the rule can act on weekends and holidays. And say what it costs WITHOUT
printing a percentage as if it were a cap: the band bounds how far a held pool can pull the rule's PRICE (band x hours of
closure), it does NOT bound the loss on a rung bought at that price -- after a real gap that loss is the gap itself
(measured 7.5-20% of the spend, audit D9-3), on at most `min(band x age, gap) / dipBps` rungs, one to three at the
first batch's numbers.

The form shows the backtest for the chosen stock and rule before the launch button -- `tools/rule_backtest.py` is the
engine -- and the token page shows the same chart to buyers.

## 3. What each screen calls (release in PR #29; regenerate before shipping)

`HedgeFunHook` is ONE contract for every launch; everything on it is keyed by the token pool's `PoolId`
(`keccak256(abi.encode(poolKey))`). The treasury and the token are still one per launch.

| screen | reads | writes |
|---|---|---|
| **Explore / home** | `factory.strategyCount()`, `strategies(i)` -> token, treasury, stock, creator; per strategy the token-page reads below; platform totals = sum of treasury stock per `stock` | -- |
| **Token page** | treasury: `health()` (gate open?, price), `bookedStock`, `buybackStock`, `unbookedStock`, `reserveUsdg`, `totalStockReceived`, `totalStockSpentOnBuybacks`, `totalBurned`, `stockEquivalentHeld`, `lotCount` + `lots(i)`, `params()`; hook: `sellRateBps(id)`, `buyRateBps(id)`, `accrued(id)`, `creatorOf(id)`, `pendingCreator(id)`; stock: `uiMultiplier()`; events `LotBooked` `ProfitTaken` `Stopped` `Buyback` (treasury), `Taxed` `Swept` (hook, filter by id) | -- |
| **Buy / sell with USDG** | quote = `eth_call` of the write itself from the user's address (there is no V4 quoter); show `sellRateBps` -- it spikes to 90% at launch and after each buy-back, decaying over 120 s | USDG `approve(router)`; `HedgeFunTradeRouter.buy(id, stockPool, usdgIn, minTokensOut, deadline)` / token `approve`, `sell(id, stockPool, tokensIn, minUsdgOut, deadline)`. `stockPool` = the deepest V3 `<stock>/USDG` pool, which is NOT always the listing's |
| **Token page, creator's metadata** | token: `getTokenInfo()` (deployer, logo, description, socials), `extraURI()`, `updatedAt()`, `locked()`, `editor()` -- pons' reader names and tuples, so a tool written for a pons token reads ours | at launch: `launchWithMetadata` writes the first entry. Later, creator or their editor: `token.setMetadata(...)`; creator only: `setEditor`, and `lock()` -- irreversible, and refused while an editor is appointed |
| **Launch** | `factory.listings(stock)` (enabled?, open price), `listingGates(stock)`, `bandCeiling(stock)`, `getDefaults()` (tax bounds, fee), `publicLaunch()` / `launchers(addr)`, `predict(q)` | fee `approve` if it is a token; `factory.launchWithMetadata(q, terms, info)` (or `launch(q, terms)` with no page), or the `HedgeFunLaunchRouter` equivalents for a first buy, a first treasury lot of stock and / or a first USDG reserve (`Capital.seedUsdg`) in the same transaction -- seed some stock too: a USDG reserve buys nothing until the price falls `dipBps` below the first booked lot. There is no hook salt to mine any more: the hook is a singleton. **Send with >= 1.25x the gas estimate** (about 7M gas with a first buy and a seed). `q.maxFee` and `q.expectedOpenPriceE18` restate what the user saw; a change in between reverts `Restated` |
| **Creator** | hook: `owed(id, me)`, `owedCreator(id)` | `claim(id, to)` (mine, to any address); anyone: `claimFor(id, who)`; `vetoCreator(id)` if a takeover was proposed |
| **Keeper (anyone, paid a bounty)** | simulate first; an action that does not revert is due | hook `sweep(id)`; treasury `book()`, `takeProfit(i)`, `stopLoss(i)`, `buyDip()`, `buyback()` |
| **Protocol (Safe only)** | `owner()`, `pendingOwner()` | `list`, `setListingGates`, `setBandCeiling`, `setLauncher`, `setPublicLaunch`, `setDefaults`; hook `setProtocol`, `proposeCreator` / `acceptCreator`; treasury `setVoteDelegate` (reserved); calendar `setOverride` (the brake). `emergency/` builds and decodes these |

**First-buy concentration is shown on every token, not left to a block explorer.** Every production listing opens at
about **$10k FDV** (the listing's `openPriceE18` x the stock's oracle price x 1e9 supply; set per stock on 2026-09-22), so a
launcher's first buy through `HedgeFunLaunchRouter` can take a large share cheaply: the seed is a constant-product curve
with virtual stock reserve `supply x openPrice`, the launcher pays only the ordinary buy tax (the snipe rate exempts the
launch's own caller), and a buy worth the opening FDV takes about 45% of supply. The owner decided to keep the low
open and make the concentration visible instead. The front end:

- **Token page and every card:** "Launcher bought X% of supply at launch", with X = `LaunchedWithCapital.tokensBought / totalSupply`
  (filter by `id`; a plain `factory.launch` has no router event and shows 0% from it). Beside it, the launcher's **current**
  balance as a share of supply, so a bag that has been sold is visible as sold.
- **Launch block:** the share of supply bought by every address in the launch transaction's block and the following
  `snipeSeconds`, from the token's `Transfer` events out of the V4 `PoolManager` (a swap event names the router, not
  the buyer), labelled "bought in the first N seconds". A launcher can split a first
  buy across wallets; the block view is what catches that.
- **Holders:** top holders as a share of supply, excluding the V4 `PoolManager` (it holds the pool's tokens) and the
  zero/dead addresses; flag any single holder above 10%.
- **Launch form:** before the creator signs, show what their first buy will take:
  `tokens = supply x x / (V + x) x (1 - taxBps/1e4)`, with `V = supply x openPriceE18 / 1e18` and `x` the stock the buy
  converts to. Show the opening FDV in dollars from the listing and the oracle, never a hard-coded $10k: it is fixed in
  stock units and drifts with the stock.

**The metadata is unvalidated user content, written by a stranger, and it can change.** Treat it as hostile:

- escape every string wherever it is rendered; never inject it as HTML;
- allowlist URL schemes -- `https:` and `ipfs:` only -- and render anything else as plain text, not a link;
- never fetch `logo` or `extraURI` from a server of ours without an allowlist and size / content-type limits (it is an
  SSRF and a tracking pixel otherwise); an image proxy with a cache is the usual answer;
- show `updatedAt` ("links changed 2 hours ago") and whether the entry is `locked` -- but `updatedAt != 0` does NOT mean
  "has a card" (writing an empty page stamps it too): decide that from the fields; an unlocked entry can be swapped for
  a phishing link after people trust it;
- the page's editor is the LAUNCH-TIME creator (`token.deployer()`), which after a payout takeover is no longer the
  address being paid (`hook.creatorOf(id)`): show both when they differ;
- keep an off-chain hide list. Nothing on chain can remove an entry, by design.

**The launch form writes the page in the launch transaction.** `factory.launchWithMetadata(q, terms, info)` (and the
matching `HedgeFunLaunchRouter` entry) hands the creator's first entry to the token as it is born, so a coin is never live with
an empty card -- the first seconds are when snipers, screeners and chat bots index it, and some never look again.
`launch(q, terms)` still exists for a launch with no page. After that the creator edits with `token.setMetadata`; the
launch-time write is one-shot and the protocol has no say afterwards.

- **Validate BYTE lengths in the form, before sending:** 256 per link (logo, five socials, `extraURI`), 1,024 for the
  description, 64 for the name, 32 for the symbol. One field over its cap reverts the WHOLE launch (`TooLong` /
  `BadRequest`), and a CJK character is three bytes -- count bytes, not characters.
- Gas: the page adds about 220k to a launch, up to ~2.2M with every field at its cap; include it before applying the
  1.25x rule.
- **Locking is three deliberate steps, in this order: dismiss the editor, re-read the entry, lock.** `lock()` refuses
  while an editor is appointed (audit K7-1): an editor -- or whoever holds its key -- could otherwise land a drainer
  link in the same block, just ahead of the creator's lock, and it would be frozen for ever. The UI shows the entry
  again after the dismissal and only then offers the irreversible button.

**The score is computed from EVENTS, never from the treasury's USDG balance.** Anyone can send USDG to any treasury, a
launch may seed some (`LaunchedWithCapital.seedUsdg`), and the on-chain `stockEquivalentHeld()` counts the whole
balance -- so a "rule vs holding" read off the balance can be BOUGHT with a donation. What the rule earned is the USDG its
take-profits and stops brought in (`ProfitTaken.usdgToReserve`, `Stopped` qty x price) less what its dip buys spent
(`LotBooked` with `fromTax = false`, qty x cost); anything in `reserveUsdg()` beyond that is shown as "seeded / donated
reserve" and is not scored. `tools/dashboard.py` does exactly this and was checked with a 50 USDG donation (score
unchanged). Donated STOCK cannot be told from tax stock on chain -- both are `LotBooked(fromTax = true)` -- so say
"received", not "earned from tax".

**Booking waits for the market.** A lot is booked only on a live Chainlink print (audit D9-1): stock that arrives over a
closure sits in `unbookedStock()` -- label it "waiting to be booked at the next open" -- and is booked at the opening
price. The launch form defaults `mustBook` to TRUE while the market is open and FALSE while it is shut (with it true a
closed-market launch reverts `NotBooked`), and says "your seed is booked at the next open, at the opening price". The
keeper calls `book()` on every strategy at each open.

`lastSalePrice()` is now the DIP REFERENCE -- the first booked lot sets it, a sale or a dip buy replaces it -- so label it
that way; it is not always a sale.

Rules for the page, from the audits: show the launcher's own first-buy share (R4-1); read the creator from the hook,
not from `strategies(i).creator`, which is a birth record; "shares" always means raw amount x `uiMultiplier` / 1e18;
never show a per-token value.
