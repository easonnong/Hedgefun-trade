## Three scoping facts that set every severity

- **V1 is live** — 9 strategies, 18 listings, read on chain 2026-09-27 — and **nothing in
  this PR can reach any of it.** `HedgeFunFactory` compiles byte-identically at both refs;
  the hook grows +873 bytes, so v2 needs its own hook at a newly mined address.
- **No v2 `Defaults` exist**, so the deployment configuration cannot be audited. Four
  graded findings live inside parameters nobody has set. This audit certifies no
  configuration.
- **Two of the six remediation budgets are already spent**: `CurveDeployer` has 176 bytes
  of margin and `HedgeFunV2Factory` has 27.

## The six Mediums

- **M-0** The LP fee turns the 90% sell spike from profit-gated into volume-gated and
  re-armable by anyone every 240 s. A 1-wei buy-back pot arms it; the "buy-back" buys
  nothing and burns nothing. Fix is zero bytes: ship `spikeBps = 0`, or gate `noteEvent()`
  on a nonzero `burned`.
- **M-1** `collectFees()` couples the stock leg to the token burn, so a refused stock
  transfer also reverts a burn that does not depend on it. The hook in this same repo
  splits exactly these two legs and says why.
- **M-2** A full v2 raise is a one-directional buy through the stock's only V3 pool; ten of
  the eighteen listings are too thin for it. The deliverable is a listing rule, not a count.
- **M-3** Buyer funds can be permanently stranded on a curve that can never graduate. INTC
  is a live instance and it is getting worse: the pool delivered 104.1%, then 115.9%, then
  126.1% short of the requirement over 2026-09-27 alone.
- **M-4** The enforced parameter floors are 6x and 11x wider than the project's own
  experiment recommends.
- **M-5 / M-6** The v2 documents' quantitative evidence does not reproduce at this ref, and
  its GO verdict is scoped to a ref at which two of the nine contracts did not exist.

- The round's **most-promoted structural conclusion was false**. "The only correct

  relayed as the round's most actionable finding; the adversarial pass rebuilt all three

- **I-11 was a false positive** on its central sentence, and the lead's tripwire synthesis
  built on the splitting argument died with it.
- No context-free lane ran this round, no source claims to have read all 1,458 new lines,
  and all six sources share one model family.
