## The three Mediums

- **M4-1** The rebalance is a predictable, permissionless, unpaid order that can be
  sandwiched inside the gates. The treasury pays about 50 bps more per action whoever
  sits at the gate's edge; on 0.05% pools the sandwicher is paid once the trade exceeds
  about a tenth of the pool's depth per 1%.
- **M4-2** The engine's configuration floors admit a 1 bp band, a 1 s cooldown and an
  unbounded day, and the creator-side setter validates nothing. A 1 bp band trades on
  every Chainlink print and leaks about 0.03–0.09% of the treasury a day on a volatile
  name, for good.
- **M4-3** Strategy gains never reach the burn — kind 2 burns 0.19% of supply over 90 days
  on every path, kind 0 up to 2.20% — and nothing a buyer reads says so.
